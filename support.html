Support

If something is not working, or you need help with your Heartsy account, email us. Include the email address you use to sign in.

Support email support@heartsyapp.com

What we can help with

  • Sign-in or account problems
  • Privacy and data requests
  • Account deletion if you cannot use the app
  • Product questions and feedback

Delete your account

In Heartsy, open Account and choose Delete Account. If you cannot open the app, email support@heartsyapp.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it.
